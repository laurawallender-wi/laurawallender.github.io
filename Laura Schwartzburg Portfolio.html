Approach

Three rules I hold every account to

Treatment center marketing has a short list of outcomes that matter: did the phone ring, did a qualified person answer, and did that person admit. Everything in the ad account is organized to answer those three questions.

Report admissions, not leads

A form fill or a 40 second call is not a result. I build the match between call tracking records and the client's intake sheet or CRM so cost per admission and return on spend are the headline numbers, with lead counts kept as diagnostics.

One call, one conversion, one meaning

Each account gets dedicated Google Ads conversion actions for qualified and highly qualified calls, scored on call quality so the bands are mutually exclusive. The Admission action means an actual admission, nothing else, so Smart Bidding learns from real outcomes.

Compliance before optimization

Behavioral health advertising carries HIPAA exposure. Enhanced Conversions, remarketing audiences, call recording, redaction and AI transcript scoring are each reviewed and signed off before they are turned on, and every account is audited against the same checklist. A voicemail coverage audit across 17 accounts surfaced a live hang-up on one client's Google Ads tracking number, fixed in the same session.

Case 01

Tying five months of Google Ads spend to confirmed admissions

Client Multi-site addiction treatment provider, CanadaPeriod April to August 2026Scope Google Ads, CTM, intake sheet match, ROI model

Situation

The client received monthly lead counts but had no view of which admissions came from paid search, organic, or referral, and no cost per admission. Spend was significant and the retainer covered both SEO and paid.

What I did

  • Matched the client's admissions list to call tracking records on caller phone number, tagging the driving call for each admission.
  • Reported first touch and last touch source side by side rather than choosing one, and stated untraced admissions separately as a coverage figure instead of hiding them in the percentages.
  • Built the ROI model with ad spend as the paid cost basis and CTM-traced admissions as the numerator, with the retainer shown only in the whole-program view.
  • Delivered an internal report with a reconciliation appendix, a client summary, and a chart dashboard with no patient identifiers.

Case 02

Reconciling paid search and business development credit for the same admissions

Client Residential treatment center, ArizonaPeriod March to September 2026Scope Google Ads, CTM, Salesforce, month over month reporting

Situation

Admissions counts from the client's CRM and from call tracking disagreed. The CRM marked an admission as referral-sourced whenever a business development relationship touched it, while call tracking credited the number that rang. The client was weighing a monthly Google Ads budget against a business development spend of similar size.

What I did

  • Built an admissions register merging the client's intake sheet, the CTM admission tag, and the admissions director's monthly report into one row-level record with status and owner columns.
  • Presented two attribution scenarios in the client report, disputed rows credited to business development versus credited to digital, rather than picking a winner.
  • Set the qualified call definition for the account (answered, unique caller, two or more minutes of talk time) and produced a one-page month over month story: answer rate, who answered, agreed-source admissions, cost per admission under each scenario.
  • Automated a weekly Salesforce admissions report to feed the CTM match going forward.

Case 03

Building a paid search and Performance Max account from zero

Client Outpatient and dual diagnosis center, Massachusetts North ShorePeriod August 2026 onwardScope Account structure, keywords, negatives, landing pages, call scoring

Situation

New account in a competitive regional market. The client's primary mental health program was not yet licensed, so ad copy could name depression and anxiety only, and dual diagnosis had to be the entry point. No brand campaign existed yet.

What I did

  • Structured the account as a Local campaign serving only geo-modified and near-me queries across the North Shore towns, a statewide generic campaign, a Mental Health campaign in four ad groups, and Performance Max.
  • Wrote paired phrase and exact match keyword lists per theme, and a brand negative list (spellings, spacing variants, domain strings) applied account-wide so PMax and non-brand Search could not claim brand demand.
  • Built two standalone, noindexed IOP landing pages as an A/B pair, flexibility-led and holistic-led, call-first with a short backup form, with the tracking pool swapping the displayed number.
  • Scoped AI transcript scoring to paid tracking numbers only, under written compliance sign-off, so the qualified and highly qualified conversion actions fire automatically from call quality.

Case 04

A four-tier conversion standard rolled out across the portfolio

Client Portfolio-wide, 14 active treatment center accountsPeriod 2025 to 2026Scope CTM triggers, GA4 custom dimensions, Google Ads conversion actions

Situation

Accounts reported a single undifferentiated "call" conversion, so a wrong number and a 20 minute admissions conversation counted the same to Smart Bidding and to the client.

What I did

  • Designed the four tiers: lead, qualified lead (4+ star score or 180 seconds), highly qualified lead (5 stars or 300 seconds), and admission, with the Google Ads side made mutually exclusive so each call produces exactly one conversion.
  • Deployed CTM to GA4 mapping across all 14 active accounts, registering custom dimensions for source and call attributes, and created dedicated qualified and highly qualified conversion actions per Google Ads account.
  • Enabled Enhanced Conversions for leads where it was missing, after compliance review.
  • Wrote the tracking standard checklist (34 items) and ran it against a live account to reconcile the written standard with the deployed implementation before freezing it.
  • Corrected organic and Google Business Profile source ordering bugs across 11 accounts, and set excluded-referrer and first-touch rules portfolio-wide so self-referrals stop claiming credit.
  • Planned the path to 100% tracking and attribution: 13 criteria across three levels (capture, outcome, value), five rollout waves, CRM-integrated accounts first, estimated bottom-up at 190 to 210 hours.

Case 05

Cost per admission by clinic for a client with no paid search program

Client Three-clinic outpatient network, IdahoPeriod April to August 2026Scope Tracking audit, admissions match by location, cost per admission

Situation

The client runs no paid search and wanted to know what each clinic's admissions cost against the marketing retainer, and which channels were producing them. The same measurement discipline used on paid accounts applies here: the retainer is the cost basis and the intake sheet is the source of truth for admissions.

What I did

  • Matched the intake running sheet (one tab per clinic) to call records and reported admissions and cost per traced admission for each location, with coverage stated plainly.
  • Audited dynamic number insertion on every page using the multi-clinic location block, from a clean session, and documented the untracked click-to-call paths.
  • Wrote the build spec to bring the account onto the four-tier standard, including the design problem that an admission tag applied weeks after a call needs an update-based trigger rather than an end-of-call trigger.
  • Reported cost per admission only, since revenue fields in the intake sheet were not populated consistently enough to support an ROI figure, and said so.
